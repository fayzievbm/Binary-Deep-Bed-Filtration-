# Comparison with the closest filtration models

Checked 25 November 2026. This note compares the completed analytical and controlled numerical extension with the closest inspected formulations. The conditional proofs are supplied in theory.md, with independent algebraic checks and numerical results in this supplement. The bounded literature search does not establish that no related result exists elsewhere.

The 2022 paper's equations (7)–(12) are a direct numerical antecedent, but their mobile storage and hydraulic closures differ from the seven-saturation formulation. Its shared-capacity rule must not be silently described as the present model's independent caps. Its published porosity relation contains particle-material-density factors; this note does not repair or simplify that printed relation.

The 2025 paper is also a close antecedent. Its publisher-version title page identifies ten pages, volume 3356 and article 020005. Its active/passive states differ from the deposit-associated liquid states in the original 2017 model. Adding a charging branch and reproducing nonmonotonic profiles would therefore not by itself establish a substantial new contribution.

## Specific novelty boundaries

**Pore-volume operating-mode equivalence.** The general time-reparameterization idea is established mathematics. The completed result states the precise homogeneity condition for the retained 2017 kinetic coefficient forms, together with all four branches, threshold events, admissibility conditions and a controlled test of equivalence breaking. In particular, positive common flux and identical inlet data as a function of injected volume are necessary parts of the stated comparison. This is not a claim to originate pore-volume scaling.

**Invariant region.** A proof must state the input restrictions under which it preserves nonnegative reconstructed carrier liquid as well as particle/deposit bounds. A capacity inequality alone does not establish every concentration bound. No global classical well-posedness or uniqueness result should be inferred merely from finite-volume conservation or generalized threshold handling.

**Conditional interior bistability at the aging threshold.** At constant concentration and static coefficients, let r=alpha*c/beta, C=Bcap and T=B2. The coexistence of a transition equilibrium B_L=r and aging equilibrium B_H=sqrt(C*r) must include admissibility and basin conditions. For example, max(B1,T^2/C)<r<T places the two candidates on opposite sides of T. At the threshold the one-sided sources point away from T, so equality conventions and admissible initial loading matter. This is an analysis of the chosen discontinuous phenomenological law, not evidence that physical deposits necessarily have hysteresis. Starting a clean bed directly at one constant final inlet does not demonstrate access to both equilibria; the completed history experiments use explicitly declared preconditioning inputs.

**Matched capacities.** If two closures reach the same fully filled independent caps with the same fixed deposit porosities and feed concentrations, their uniform terminal mobile fraction, permeability and viscosity coincide. A lasting terminal-flow advantage cannot then be attributed to stage history. Relevant differences concern attainability, approach times, breakthrough and spatial resistance before terminal filling. This algebraic observation should be combined with the conditional theorem and computed control results rather than promoted as a major theorem alone.

## Targeted search and access limits

The full supplied 2017 article and the supplied IJAM 2020 article were checked locally. The 2022 model, numerical section, results and conclusion were inspected in an author-deposited primary publisher manuscript. The 2025 publisher-version article was inspected through its author's ResearchGate deposit. The open Applied Sciences 2020 article was checked for closely related conclusions, including equilibrium before capacity and nonmonotonic behavior.

No explicit bistability/hysteresis or operating-mode/PV-equivalence claim was identified in these closest texts. Targeted text searches for bistability and hysteresis in the accessible 2020/2025 texts found no matching terms. The earlier 2022 author-copy route sometimes timed out; an alternate author-copy URL exposed its full eight-page text. Two targeted broader searches on filtration hysteresis/bistability and pore-volume time rescaling did not establish a direct prior result for this specific branch law. This is a bounded search, not an exhaustive novelty certification. Related filtration-front and deposition-mode theory exists and broad first-ever claims are inappropriate.
