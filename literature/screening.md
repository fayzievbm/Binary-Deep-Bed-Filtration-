# Screening of the 137 Scopus AI references

All 137 reference titles and their supplied bibliographic context were screened. This is a relevance screen, not a claim that every full text was read. Selected borderline sources were checked against primary publisher abstracts or primary-paper references. Scopus AI prose is treated as a discovery aid, not as citable scientific evidence.

## Conclusion for the introduction and literature review

No additional directly matched binary deep-bed multistage source was found outside the set the parent is already verifying. Retain the focused sources already selected, especially 8, 58 and 109. Their full texts are more important to the novelty assessment than enlarging the review with generic multiphase-flow citations. Reference 8 has essentially the same title as the proposed work and needs an explicit overlap analysis before any novelty claim.

The list also omits several already-known central works: the supplied Makhmudov et al. 2017 base model, the supplied Symmetry 2020 paper, the supplied IJAM2020 paper, and Gitis et al. 2010. Their absence reinforces that the generated list is not a systematic or complete review.

## Principal conflations to remove

- Two suspended solid particle populations in one carrier liquid are not two immiscible fluids. Here mobile and immobilized liquid are storage categories for the same carrier; they are not oil/water or gas/water phases separated by a moving interface.
- Buckley-Leverett displacement, capillary pressure, saturation-dependent relative permeability, fluid-fluid interfacial area, NAPL dissolution and trapped-gas mass transfer cannot establish missing mechanisms or required terms for this specific seven-saturation suspension model.
- Two-site sorption and two-region solute exchange are useful conceptual analogies. They are not evidence that dissolved tracers and retained solid particles follow the same kinetic closure.
- Molecular deposition/epitaxy, vapor-grown films, gecko adhesion and nanoparticle self-assembly are not deep-bed suspension filtration merely because they use the word deposition.
- External cake, cross-flow membrane and fibrous-filter results must be distinguished from internal retention in a granular porous bed.
