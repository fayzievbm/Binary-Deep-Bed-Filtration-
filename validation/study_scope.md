# Study completed with available published observations

The primary result is a mathematical and numerical comparison within the 2017
seven-saturation filtration framework. Only the deposition kinetic closure
changes. The balance laws, fixed deposit porosities, liquid exchange, Darcy
law, permeability and viscosity remain unchanged.

All published-data comparisons use existing digitized observations and recorded
simulations. The manuscript neither depends on correspondence nor assumes that
additional measurements will become available. No new laboratory measurements
or newly supplied run metadata enter this revision.

Gitis Figure 6 is a conditional normalized-response comparison. The proportional
mapping from its plotted R to model c/c0, inlet scale, deposit porosity and
startup offset are explicit assumptions. Related 2008/2005 experimental papers
provide context, not replacement inputs to the 2010 runs. Figure 7 remains an
unsuccessful transfer diagnostic under incompletely specified run conditions.

Alem provides independently examined concentration, retention and hydraulic
quantities. The tested multistage parameterization fits the constant-flow
concentration history worse than linear kinetics; both underestimate hydraulic
ratios. The mismatch persists when the four overlapping permeability symbols
are excluded: the three remaining observations give RMSE0.121156 and0.130485.
This subset is included in the original seven observations, not a new dataset.
The nominal inventory-based hydraulic bound remains conditional on consistent
measurement domain, endpoint and normalization. It is not a statistical
rejection without experimental uncertainty bounds.

Numerical verification, observational agreement and material-parameter
identification are separate results. The binary behavior, aging branch and
terminal arrest have been explored numerically. Their complete experimental
validation is not claimed.
