The paper also states in its sampling procedure that care was taken to verify no external cake. This is compatible with choosing the lower-loading data for internal/depth deposition; it does not prove the assumptions about fixed deposit porosity or the2017 permeability relation.

The hydraulic bound should therefore be reported as a consistency test conditional on the paper's overall-permeability interpretation and the unchanged2017 constitutive assumptions. Do not interpret it as a measurement of deposit porosity or as a universal physical impossibility.
