## Methods addition: conditional parameter-information diagnostics

Parameter information is examined using only the original nine Gitis calibration symbols and 26 Alem constant-flow concentration symbols. No held-out response enters these diagnostics. At the archived multistage parameter values, normalized-response sensitivities are computed in natural-log coordinates and divided by the RMS calibration response. Singular-value decompositions use central-difference steps 0.02, 0.01 and 0.005. Gitis uses $(\alpha_s^*,\chi,B_1/c_0)$; Alem uses $(\alpha_s^*,B_{\mathrm{cap}})$ with detachment fixed. An additional Alem screen includes the additive coordinate $\beta_s^*\mathrm{PV}_{\mathrm{end}}$, with a forward difference from the near-boundary fit. This prevents a small logarithmic detachment derivative near zero from being interpreted as insensitivity over the full nonnegative range. Gitis calculations use 160 cells and Alem calculations 120 cells. Fixed-parameter checks use 320 and 240 cells, respectively.

Selected residual-objective profiles fix the Gitis charging threshold and reoptimize attachment and charging from two starting points; fix Alem capacity and reoptimize attachment and detachment within the original search bounds; or fix Alem detachment, including zero, and reoptimize attachment. These conditional local searches do not replace the archived fits or certify global minima. In the absence of experimental variances, the profiles are descriptive squared-residual diagnostics, not likelihood-based confidence intervals or significance tests. Perturbations of inactive thresholds and a common rescaling of the assumed Gitis inlet concentration and deposit thresholds are also evaluated without refitting.

## Results addition: stage activation and parameter information

The Gitis charging-threshold profile has its lowest sampled RMSE near the archived $B_1/c_0=80.5335$: the RMSE is 0.006710 at that value, 0.034812 at half and 0.043500 at double, after reoptimizing attachment and charging. This constrains the normalized transition location within the stated observation mapping, clock and input assumptions; it does not establish unconditional parameter uniqueness. The inactive aging and capacity perturbations leave the nine predictions exactly unchanged. Rescaling $c_0$ and all deposit thresholds together from $10^{-7}$ to $10^{-5}$ changes the normalized predictions by at most $9.16\times10^{-5}$, while maximum retained saturation changes from 0.0001531 to 0.0153066. Absolute loading is therefore poorly informed by these normalized responses under the specified mapping. The scaling is approximate because the original mobile-volume and hydraulic relations depend on absolute loading.

For Alem, capacities from 0.01 to 0.04 yield reoptimized attachment coefficients from 0.33517 to 0.08270, while their product remains between 0.003308 and 0.003384 and calibration RMSE between 0.090170 and 0.091769. All runs remain below capacity. The least-sensitive attachment/capacity direction has nearly opposite logarithmic components, consistent with the product in the active aging source. Early branches can break this symmetry; the calculation is not an exact global nonidentifiability proof. Setting detachment to zero instead of $10^{-7}$ changes reoptimized RMSE by only $5.9\times10^{-7}$, whereas fixing $\beta_s^*=0.001$ increases it to 0.097199. The small zero-versus-positive difference is below the $5.26\times10^{-4}$ fixed-parameter RMSE drift from 120 to 240 cells. Neither a physical preference for exactly zero detachment nor a ranking of nearby capacities is resolved. Fixed-parameter calibration RMSE changes from 0.006710 to 0.007267 for Gitis and from 0.090560 to 0.091086 for Alem under the respective mesh checks.

The finite-difference Jacobians do not demonstrate step convergence: the second Gitis singular value changes from 1.184 to 2.225 to 3.957 as the step decreases, and the smaller Alem attachment/capacity singular value changes from 0.03845 to 0.04139 to 0.11027. They are finite-step numerical sensitivity screens rather than converged derivative estimates. The opposing Alem attachment/capacity direction persists, but no precise rank cutoff or statistical parameter interval is inferred.

## Discussion addition

The parameter-information screens distinguish a reproducible normalized fit from identification of complete physical kinetics. Gitis informs only the active charging/transition calculation under an unverified proportional observation mapping. Alem supplies a near attachment–capacity ridge and a boundary detachment estimate within a closure that still fails the joint hydraulic comparison. These results neither validate the inactive stages nor resolve the unavailable mixed-population experiment. Numerical conservation, analytical results and refinement verify properties of the stated model; they do not supply the missing experimental evidence.
